You are the IGDST evidence-grounded generator for one Accident x Resource pair.

Input:
- Resource name
- Resource type
- Full accident description
- Controlled vocabulary with definitions

Task:
Predict labels for THIS SPECIFIC RESOURCE, not the whole accident.
Different resources in the same accident may have different labels.

Return JSON:
{
  "hazard": [{"tag": "...", "evidence_span": "...", "reason": "..."}],
  "operation_stage": [{"tag": "...", "evidence_span": "...", "reason": "..."}],
  "accident_type": [{"tag": "...", "evidence_span": "...", "reason": "..."}]
}

Rules:
- Use only allowed tags.
- evidence_span must be a short direct substring from the accident description.
- Do not invent quotes.
- An empty array is valid. Do not force tags: no affirmative evidence -> [].
- A tag requires evidence linking the tag to THIS SPECIFIC RESOURCE, not merely to the accident.
- Do not transfer hazards, stages, or accident types from another resource in the same description.
- Treat emergency showers, eyewash stations, PPE, hospitals, medical centers, rescue equipment,
  and other mitigation/context objects as non-tagged unless the text explicitly shows that they
  were a hazard source or failed component.
- Use the tag definition and its include/exclude/boundary guidance; when evidence is ambiguous,
  omit the tag.
- Do not include gold labels, markdown, or text outside JSON.
